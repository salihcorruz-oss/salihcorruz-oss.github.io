Privacy Policy — Vivid Stripe

Effective date: September 20, 2026

App: Vivid Stripe
Package name: com.vividstripe.game
Developer: Salih Çörüz (ClickWise)

Vivid Stripe is an Android game with three modes: Vivid Stripe (endless runner), Vivid Survival, and Vivid Puzzle. Gameplay works on your device. This policy explains what information is handled when you use the app.

1. Data stored on your device

The app may store the following locally on your device (not on our servers):

  • Scores, coins, and campaign / chapter progress for each mode
  • Unlocked and equipped runners, skins, and Survival loadout items
  • Settings (language, sound, haptics, local player name)
  • Consumable item counts and queued scores waiting to sync

We do not require an account to play. Progress is saved on the device. We do not operate our own cloud save. Optional Google Play Games sign-in can send scores to Google leaderboards (see section 5).

2. Information we do not collect directly

We do not knowingly collect through the app:

  • Your real name, email address, or phone number (unless you email us)
  • Photos, contacts, or precise GPS location
  • Health or financial information

If you contact us by email, we only receive what you choose to send.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free Android app may show ads served by Google AdMob, including:

  • Interstitial ads after some chapters or runs
  • Optional rewarded ads (for example, a continue or a Survival HUD unlock)

To show and measure those ads, AdMob and its partners may collect:

  • Advertising ID
  • Device and app identifiers, IP address, and other device information
  • Approximate location (derived from IP; we do not request precise GPS)
  • Ad views, clicks, and related interaction data

This processing is done by Google according to Google’s Privacy Policy and AdMob’s disclosures. We do not receive your Advertising ID on our own servers.

In the EEA/UK, Google’s UMP consent form may be shown. You can limit ad personalization in Android and Google account settings.

4. Push notifications (Firebase Cloud Messaging)

On Android Play Store builds the app uses Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) so we can send update notices (for example, that a new version is on Play).

FCM / Google may process:

  • A notification token (device registration token) used to deliver messages
  • Device and app information needed to route the message

The app subscribes the install to an “updates” topic. We do not run our own notification server and we do not upload the token to a ClickWise database. Tokens and delivery are handled by Google Firebase under Firebase’s privacy information and Google’s Privacy Policy.

On Android 13 and later the system may ask for notification permission. You can turn notifications off in device settings. This does not affect gameplay.

5. Google Play Games Services

Sign-in is optional. You can play without a Google account. If you choose Play Games sign-in, Google may process:

  • Play Games player ID
  • Display name (and avatar, if Google provides it)
  • Leaderboard scores you submit for Vivid Stripe, Vivid Survival, and Vivid Puzzle

Scores are sent to Google Play Games leaderboards, not to our servers. We do not use Play Games Saved Games / cloud save. We do not receive your Google email or password.

Play Games processing is described in Google’s Privacy Policy and Play Games terms. You can disconnect Play Games from your Google account settings.

6. In-app shop

Runners and gear in the shop are bought with coins earned in the game. Those purchases stay on the device. We do not receive payment card details.

7. Internet use

Core gameplay, local scores, and the coin shop work offline. Internet is used for:

  • Google AdMob ads
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging (update notices)
  • Google Play Games sign-in and leaderboards, when you use them
  • Opening the Play Store listing (for example, Check Updates)

8. Children

Vivid Stripe is intended for ages 13 and up. It is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, contact us and we will respond.

9. Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. Data may be processed by Google (AdMob, Firebase Cloud Messaging, Google Play Games, Google Play) under their policies, and by us only if you email us. On-device game data is not uploaded to ClickWise servers.

10. Your control

  • Sound, haptics, and language can be changed in the app.
  • Clearing app storage or uninstalling deletes local scores, coins, and progress.
  • You can limit ad personalization in device and Google settings.
  • You can disable notifications in Android settings.
  • Play Games sign-in is optional; you can stop using it in Google / Play Games settings.

11. Data retention

Local app data remains on your device until you clear storage or uninstall. Ad, FCM, and Play Games partners follow their own retention policies. We do not keep copies of your game save on our servers.

12. Changes

We may update this policy. The effective date at the top will change when we do.
